Pass of 2026-10-29 (edits Kieran approved; no new mechanism):
- Section 5.3 (Constraint Ceilings): "a type the DS does not recognize" removed from the malformed list. A constraint of an unrecognized type is carried and never treated as absent; a child covers it only with a constraint of the same `key` and type whose JCS serialization (RFC 8785) is byte-identical to the parent's; any exercise under it is denied (fail closed). This matches Sections 4.2 and 4.3 and the `unknown_constraint` row (step 8) of Table 2 of draft-asor-wimse-agent-delegation-chain-02 (https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-asor-wimse-agent-delegation-chain-02.txt).
