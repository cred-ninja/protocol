Design questions:
- Per-key mode marker: not added. The constraint type already carries the measurement scope (`max` per exercise, `max_lifetime` per token, `max_subtree` per subtree), and ASOR -03 records that scope per type in its registry request. What a marker would add is a guard against a producer putting a running count under `max`, which a verifier cannot detect. Section 5.3 now says so, citing ASOR -02 Section 4.2.
- S+L admission bound (public thread: https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/msg/wimse/1QcbdRQ5685edwOIDF_pW0euoAw/): S is the maximum age of the authorization state a permit is issued from, L the maximum permit lifetime; with both enforced, S + L bounds admission of new actions on pre-revocation state after revocation takes effect at the authoritative source. In this document the DS is that source and re-checks revocation from its own records at every issuance (Section 5.4), so S is zero for tokens it issues. Mediated calls stop within the Section 7 bounds; an exchanged or Section 5.5 token admits new actions until its `exp`. Section 7 already says this, so no S+L text is added. Open (decision 2): the 1-hour cap of Section 10.6 is stated for Delegation Tokens; whether it also caps exchanged and Section 5.5 tokens, which would make L at most one hour, is not stated.
